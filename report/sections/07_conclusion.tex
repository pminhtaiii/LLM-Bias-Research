\section{Conclusion}
Across ten LLM conditions on the complete BBQ benchmark, uncertainty handling emerges as a major source of behavioral variation. The local models show that strong disambiguated accuracy can coexist with weak ambiguous-context behavior, while the GPT medium/light conditions show the reverse pattern. Physical Appearance and Age are recurring ambiguous-bias hotspots, and category-level alignment gaps reveal additional localized effects.

The main methodological lesson is that BBQ should be interpreted jointly through AMB accuracy, DIS accuracy, $\samb$, $\sdis$, and alignment behavior. Near-zero directional bias does not guarantee appropriate uncertainty handling, and high QA accuracy does not rule out stereotype-sensitive behavior. Future work should add uncertainty estimates and repeat the GPT evaluation under a fully documented inference protocol.
