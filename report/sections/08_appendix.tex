\section{Reproducibility Notes}
The report tables and figures are generated from the canonical CSV files stored under \texttt{data/}. Figure-generation scripts are stored under \texttt{scripts/}, while generated figures and LaTeX tables are stored under \texttt{figures/} and \texttt{tables/}, respectively. The project-level benchmark repository is locked to the BBQ commit recorded in the methodology. For the accompanying research repository, the intended workflow is to rebuild canonical results first, then regenerate report figures from the result CSVs, and finally compile this LaTeX report with XeLaTeX.
