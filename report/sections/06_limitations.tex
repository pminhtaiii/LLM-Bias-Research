\section{Limitations}
The study has four main limitations. First, BBQ measures stereotype-sensitive question answering in U.S. English-speaking contexts and does not establish general fairness across cultures, languages, or downstream tasks \citep{parrish2022bbq}. Second, the evaluation conditions were produced under heterogeneous inference protocols; the local panel shares a common setup, Qwen uses a different prompt/runtime, and the GPT inference procedure is incomplete. Third, per-example raw outputs were not retained for the three local GGUF models, while the GPT high-condition sequences are anomalously identical and gold-perfect. Fourth, the present analysis is descriptive: we do not report confidence intervals or statistical significance tests for category-level bias or alignment gaps.
